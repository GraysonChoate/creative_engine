In this tutorial, we'll walk through exactly how we made five cinematic scenes inside Higgsfield using Seedance 2.5 — without building a single 3D setup by hand. GPT's Astra writes the Blender blocking for us, and Higgsfield turns that blocking into the finished .

Before You Start

Every case here is the same two-step pipeline: Astra blocks the scene in Blender (or 3D Jutsu, right in your browser) with simple grey shapes that lock the camera, timing and staging — then that blocking drives Seedance 2.5 as a video reference. That's what holds a 30-second continuous take together; text alone would fall apart.

Setup takes a minute: download the Blender plugin from Higgsfield, drag it into Blender, log in — then connect everything in chat with @higgsfield /use-blender. From there Astra builds scenes in Blender and sends generations to Higgsfield on its own.

Case 1 — The Infinite Loop

A man walks into a room, finds a miniature of that same room on the table with a tiny version of himself inside, panics and hides — and the camera pulls back to reveal the whole room is itself a miniature. The last frame lands exactly on the first, so the video loops forever.

The Blocking

Astra built the entire 30-second previz from a single request: the room, the nested live miniatures, the character action, and an editable handheld camera path — then verified the loop with numbers, not by eye. A second pass replaced only the ending with the pullback into a larger copy of the room, turning the loop into an endless zoom-out.
