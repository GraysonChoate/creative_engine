# Storyboard (spine step 4): six tracks, one clock

## Tracks
| Track | What's on it |
|---|---|
| **Beat** | The job of this moment: hook · setup · tension · proof · payoff · CTA |
| **Visual** | What the camera (or page) shows: framing, action, cut, scroll motion |
| **Dialogue** | Exact words, who, how (tone, cadence, pauses) |
| **Graphics** | Captions, motion graphics, callouts, UI |
| **Sound** | Room tone, foley, music, SFX, voice treatment |
| **Product** | When the product is on screen, how held, label state |

## The clock
Video: seconds. Carousel: slides. Site: scroll positions (section tops). App: screens.

## Lead track by format
| Format | Leads | Follows | Off or light |
|---|---|---|---|
| UGC selfie | Dialogue | Visual, Sound | Graphics (captions only) |
| UGC multi-scene | Dialogue | Visual cuts follow the words | Graphics light |
| Cinematic ad / reel | Visual | Sound, Graphics | Dialogue sparse or none |
| Explainer | Dialogue (VO) | Visual, Graphics | — |
| Motion carousel | Graphics | Beat | Sound optional |
| Animated / interactive ad | Graphics | Sound (UI SFX) | Dialogue rare |
| Website | Beat | Visual, Graphics | Dialogue = copy per section |
| App | Beat | Graphics (UI) | — |

Rule: the lead track is written first; the other tracks are fitted to it. UGC: words first, shots to match. Cinematic: shots first, sound and words to match.

## Story check (before tickets; engine fixes, user is not asked)
- Hook by 2 s / slide 1 / section 1
- One tension
- One proof (product doing its job)
- One payoff
- One CTA, last
- No beat longer than 25% of the runtime
- Product on screen at proof and payoff at minimum

## Board format
```
BOARD: <job> · <engine> · <mode> · runtime <n> · lead track <x>
| t | Beat | Visual | Dialogue | Graphics | Sound | Product |
| 0–2 | Hook | ... | ... | ... | ... | ... |
```
Each row becomes 1–3 tickets (tickets.md). Rows and tickets share IDs (R1 → T1a, T1b).

## Shown together
The user sees the board with its tickets and the credit total as one review. One reply approves, edits lines, or cuts lines.
