# Intake (spine step 1, with step 2 and 3 folded in)

## Order
1. **Listen.** Fill every field the user already covered. Never re-ask one.
2. **Research** (step 2). The product, the format, the brand. Output: a half-page brief with 3–5 recommendations the questions will carry.
3. **Ask the gaps.** Max 5 questions. Each has a proposed answer and a one-line reason. "Go with your picks" is always a valid reply.
4. **Directions** (step 3). Three, safe → bold, one mood snapshot each (a still, a palette, 3 words). User picks one.
5. Hand to step 4 (storyboard.md).

## The 5 universal questions (Core)
| # | Question | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Where will it run? (TikTok, Reels, YouTube, site hero, Meta ad, all) | Sets every size, safe zone, length band |
| 2 | What should the viewer do or feel at the end? | Sets the CTA and the beat track |
| 3 | Who or what must be in it? (locked person, product, line, location) | Sets the locks |
| 4 | Budget ceiling for this job? | Sets model tier, takes, resolution |
| 5 | Anything the client said is non-negotiable? | Goes in clients/<brand>/client-rules.md; checked at step 7 |

Engines add their own gap questions in their own intake.md. Core + engine together still obey the max of 5 asked.

## Four asking rules
1. Never ask what can be derived. Sizes, resolution, safe zones, file limits come from export-table.md.
2. Never ask what research answered. Propose it.
3. Max 5 questions per gate.
4. Every question has a default.

## Derived, never asked
Aspect ratios · resolution · safe zones · caption position · file size · codec · loop or not (from placement) · platform length band (proposed, not asked).

## Proposed, not asked (with reason)
Length · hook angle · pacing · CTA placement · setting (3 options) · number of scenes.

## Example: "UGC reel, Sarah, LIV creatine, selfie style, short"
Already given: engine, cast, product, frame style, roughly short. Research: creatine reels, LIV creatine page.
Asked (4):
- Setting: car before the gym or home kitchen? I'd do the car; top creatine reels in this niche are pre-gym.
- Length: 22 s? Niche winners run 18–25 s and hook by second 2.
- Hook: (a) "I almost quit" (b) "the one thing I was missing" (c) "3 weeks in". I'd go (c), results lead in creatine.
- Still, or mid-action (grabbing the tub, shaking it)? I'd do mid-action; stillness reads staged.
Then 3 directions, then the board.
