# CE-07 PITCH DECK (creative concept deck)

Run this when the job is a deck to win ideas, inspiration and feedback. It assembles work from ce-00, ce-04/05, ce-06 and others. It does not make new films.
Works for any project: website rebuild, campaign, film, product launch. Variables: [BRAND], [GOAL], [LENGTH: set by me], [DIRECTIONS: how many looks to show].

## LENGTH
I set the length each time ("6 slides", "short", "full"). Follow it exactly. If I do not say, use 10 and say so. To shorten, cut or merge from the bottom of the module list. To lengthen, add modules marked optional. The slide count is never a rule of this skill.

## MODE
- EXPLORE (default): draft fast, no gates. ALWAYS ON: no invented claims, reviews, quotes or numbers about the brand; mark every inferred item "from your site, please confirm"; real screenshots, real logo and real packs only; no live site or DNS; no credentials; quote credits before spend.
- BUILD: same, plus approval at the outline and a final check pass by a separate agent. Use when I say "client", "send" or "ship".

## Inputs (ask once, only what is missing)
[BRAND URL] [what exists: bible, audit, boards, films, links] [who sees the deck] [length]

## Steps
1. **Gather.** Use what exists. No bible: run ce-00 light intake from the website. No audit: run the ce-04/05 audit. Do not remake finished work.
2. **Outline (show me, then stop).** One line per slide, chosen from the modules below to fit the length. I approve or change it.
3. **Build.** Create the deck from the Slides artifact type when it is listed (Artifact quickstart, intent slides). Otherwise make a .pptx with the pptx skill. Real screenshots and images only. Text in code, never baked into generated images.
4. **Check.** Look at every slide as an image. Fix overflow, tiny text, wrong logos or labels, anything inferred but not marked. Then show me.
5. **Hand over.** The deck, plus a one-line list of what is inferred and what we still need.

## Modules (pick and order to fit; most important first)
1. **Idea.** The one line the work says.
2. **What we saw.** Real screenshots of the current state (phone and desktop). What works, what does not.
3. **Brand as we read it.** Palette, fonts, voice, products. Marked inferred if not from a brand guide.
4. **Directions.** Tame / new direction / spectacular, one board each (palette, type, sketch). Ask them to pick.
5. **Storyboard.** Section by section (site) or scene by scene (film), with their real products.
6. **Motion proof.** Finished films or scroll pieces: a still and a link or short loop each.
7. **What we need.** Brand guide, logos, product photos, copy, access, who approves.
8. **Questions and next steps.** 3 specific questions, rough timeline.
Optional: **Category snapshot** (competitors: match / beat), **Site map**, **Principles** (3 rules the work follows), **Process**, **Case study**, **Pricing** (only if asked).
Order follows the story: where they are, what we think, what we could do, what we need.

## Rules
- Inferred is not fact. Label it. Never write copy as if the client said it.
- One idea per slide. About 20 words or fewer. Big images. 2 to 3 fonts.
- Ask for reactions in a form that gets answers: pick A, B or C; Love / Change / Cut on each slide.
- Do not imply approval or partnership by using their name or logo.
- Videos stay as links or short loops; do not bloat the deck.
- Camera, effects and world logic for any imagery: START-HERE.md at github.com/GraysonChoate/creative_engine.
- Do not carry one project's choices (length, order, modules) into the next. Ask or follow the new request.

## Failure modes -> fix
| Problem | Fix |
|---|---|
| Deck looks generic | Use their real screenshots and real products; cut stock-looking art. |
| Too many words | One idea per slide; move detail to speaker notes. |
| Looks like final design | Label directions "direction, not final". |
| No brand guide yet | Brand module says inferred; needs module asks for it. |
| Too long | Merge or cut from the bottom of the module list. |

Output style: short, plain language. Ask at most one question at a time.
