# Shot and Effect Playbook (v1, grows with every new video)

How to use: find the move you want, copy the prompt phrase, and give it a real cause. Effects need a real trigger (see START-HERE rule 4). Numbers beat adjectives.

| Move / effect | Plain words | Prompt phrase (from the research) | Real cause example | Watch out |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Macro zoom | Fly from wide into a tiny detail | "the lens zooms in" with start and end FOV in degrees, e.g. "FOV widening gradually and evenly from 29° to 107°" | Follow her hands tying a shoe, end inside the thread weave | Keep the same shoe and light from start to end |
| Optical zoom only | Camera stays put, only the lens changes | "The camera holds one fixed spot the whole time; only the lens focal length changes" | Zoom on the bottle cap from across the kitchen | Do not also move the camera |
| Dolly zoom | Background warps behind a still subject | "the lens zooms in while the camera pulls away" | Runner stops at the trailhead, world stretches | Use once per ad |
| Flythrough via occlusion | One unbroken shot passing through objects | "ONE continuous shot with no cuts: all transitions are camera moves through physical occlusions" | Pass through steam, a door, a swinging towel | Each hand-off needs a real object that fills the frame |
| Camera follows an object | Camera whips onto a thrown thing and rides it | "the camera whips onto the @cannonball and FLIES WITH IT" | Camera rides the cap as it spins off the bottle | One object, one path |
| Robo-arm punch | Hard vertical or push move, then brake | "The camera acts like a robo-arm: punches through the floor vertically and brakes hard." | Drop from rooftop to her kitchen window | Brake before the cut |
| Speed ramp | Snap in, sag in the middle, accelerate out | "snap in hard, sag in the middle and accelerate into the cut" | Each product reveal | Do not ramp every shot |
| Real handheld | Slow body sway plus tiny tremor | "a lazy body sway in long waves ... plus a very small tremor"; or "a constant fine 1-2cm tremor" | Morning run follow shot | Not a wiggle |
| Deep parallax | Foreground, middle, far all move at different speeds | "foreground ... rush past, midground ... slide by, the far canopy and sky shift slowly" | Trail run, leaves in front | Name all three layers |
| Seamless loop / Droste | Last frame equals first frame, pull-through | Same first and last frame; "the mirror is an open doorway into an exact mirrored copy of the room" | Hero background loop | Verify the loop with numbers, not by eye |
| Liquid / product swirl (hypermotion) | Product or liquid moves in impossible shapes | "hypermotion, CGI commercial video of our products"; leave liquids black in the blockout, then fill | She opens the bottle, liquid rises around her | Liquid starts at the bottle, ends in the glass or bottle |
| Dust / powder burst | Particles carry the camera into the next shot | "Ending the shot inside the dust is what sets up the seamless transition" | Scoop of powder hits the shaker | End-state lock: last frame inside the dust |
| Pack shot push-in | Final product hero | Dolly-in or slow pullback; light matches scene 1; logo added in post | Bottle on kitchen counter, morning light | Product sits on a surface with a contact shadow |
| Logo reveal | Real logo animates in | "elegant dynamic logo reveal animation for the logo in Image 1 ... Liquid Glass aesthetic" | End card | Use the real logo file |
| Screen-in-scene | Real UI on a device inside the shot | "The TV picture reads as a real physical panel being filmed, not a clean digital overlay" | Her phone showing the app | Match duration, add glare |

## Words to say (your words to model words)
- "Zoom into the threads" = macro zoom with start and end focal length.
- "Fly through" = occlusion transition.
- "Make it swirl" = hypermotion with a named real source.
- "Feels shaky and real" = real handheld.
- "Looks deep" = parallax with three named layers.
