# CE-05 EXPLORE / BUILD mode (decided 2026-10-06)

Why: strict gates and checks suppressed early experiments. Fix: two modes.

## EXPLORE (default for experiments, demos, pitches)
- No gates, audits, weight budgets, brand checks, fallbacks, delivery.
- Keep craft only: locks, previews, loops, lighting, short beats, one-line fixes.
- ALWAYS ON: quote credits; no real person's likeness without consent; no fake reviews or claims shown as real; sites: no touching a live site or DNS, no credentials.
- Spectacular tier is the default for ce-04 / ce-05.

## BUILD (full gated workflow)
Trigger words: "build", "ship", "client", or picking a winner.
Items deferred to BUILD: scroll playback export, mobile weight and fallback, 3D export (website gap).

## Revised real-label rule
AI may copy label text from a real reference. Text comes only from the real source. Every result is checked against the real image; on a fail, put the real label back or re-run. Never invent text, logos or products for a real brand. Never generate fake before/after proof. Prefer real images as references for new renders (light and shadow built in) over flat cutouts; keep the cutout composite as backup when the label must be exact. Video and small print garble most.
